FAQ

What's the difference between friendly and ranked matches?

Friendly: you send a join request and the creator accepts or declines. It affects your level but not your rating.

Ranked: you join instantly, without approval, if your rating is within 200 points of the creator's. It affects both your level and your rating.

What's the difference between level and rating?

Level (1.0 to 7.0) estimates how you perform and changes based on the share of games you win compared to what was expected. Rating is points that change with wins and losses in ranked matches only.

Why did my level drop even though I won?

Level measures performance, not just winning. If you beat a weaker player by a smaller margin than expected, your level may drop slightly. Full details are under the (i) icon next to "Level" on your profile.

How do I submit a match result?

After the match time, open My matches, tap Enter result and enter each set. A player from the other team then confirms it.

What if my opponent doesn't confirm the result?

If it isn't confirmed or disputed within 24 hours of submission, it is confirmed automatically.

My opponent submitted a wrong result. What do I do?

Tap Dispute in My matches within 24 hours of submission. The result is put on hold for the admins to review, and their decision is final.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. You'll get a link to set a new password. If it doesn't arrive, check your Junk folder.

How do I leave or cancel a match?

From My matches: if you created the match you can cancel it, and if you joined you can leave. Both are available until the match time, and the other players are notified.

Why can't I join a ranked match?

Because your rating is more than 200 points away from the creator's. Try friendly matches or create your own.

Can I request more than one match at the same time?

You can send requests to several matches at the same time, and as soon as you're accepted into one, your other requests are withdrawn automatically. But you can't be in two matches less than an hour apart, whether you created them or joined. For example, 7:00 and 8:00 is fine, but 7:30 and 8:00 is not.

How do I edit my profile or change language and appearance?

Go to Profile → Edit profile to change your name, age and region. Language and appearance are in Profile too. When browsing matches, you can show another region with the region button.

Is the level in the app an official rating?

No. It's the app's own internal estimate, and the app is independent and not affiliated with any federation.

How do I report a player?

Open their profile, tap Report player and choose a reason. Reports go straight to the admins.

How do I block a player?

Open their profile and tap Block player. You won't see their matches or messages, they can't request or join your matches, and they aren't notified. If you have an upcoming match with them, you can leave or cancel it. To unblock, go to Profile → Blocked players.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. Deletion is permanent, and you can create a new account later.

Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free. Court booking costs are agreed between players.

Nidd is an independent app. It is not affiliated with any federation and is not an official rating body.
