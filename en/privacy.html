Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the Nidd app collects, how we use and protect it, and your rights over it. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Email address: used only to sign in, activate your account and reset your password. Your password is stored encrypted by Firebase, and we can't see it.
  • Profile: name, age, gender and region.
  • Sports data: the level you pick, your calculated level, rating, matches played, wins, losses and level history.
  • Matches: dates, courts, results, join requests and disputes.
  • Chats: messages inside each match chat.
  • Reports and in-app notifications, and the list of players you've blocked (visible only to you).
  • Device settings such as language and appearance, stored on your device only.

We do not collect your location, contacts or any payment data.

2. How we use your data

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To show suitable matches and connect you with players at your level.
  • To calculate levels and ratings after each confirmed match and show the ranking.
  • To send in-app notifications, such as an accepted request or a full match.
  • To protect users, review reports and disputes, and prevent abuse and manipulation.
  • To reply when you contact us.

3. What other players can see

Signed-in players can see your name, age, gender, region, level, rating, stats, level history and the matches you created or joined.

A match chat is visible only to that match's players, and the admins may review it when handling a report or dispute.

Your email address is never shown to other players.

4. Sharing data

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

We use Google Firebase to host data and handle sign-in, and your data may be processed on servers outside Saudi Arabia under applicable protection standards.

We may disclose data when required by a competent authority under applicable law.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account. When you do:

  • Your profile, stats, notifications and join requests are deleted.
  • You are removed from your upcoming matches, and your spot opens for other players.
  • Matches already played stay in the records of the players you played with, under your name, so their records and levels are not affected.
  • Reports linked to your account may be kept for a limited time for safety purposes.

6. Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • Know what data we collect about you and how we use it.
  • Access your data and request a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data by deleting your account.

For any of these requests, contact us at the email below.

7. Data security

We protect your data with access rules that stop any player from reading or changing other players' data, and connections to our servers are encrypted. Still, no online system is 100% secure.

8. Age

The app is for people aged 13 and over. Users under 18 must use the app with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will publish the update in the app with its date. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept it.

10. Contact

For any question about your privacy or data, email us at: wherematchesbegin@outlook.com

Nidd is an independent app. It is not affiliated with any federation and is not an official rating body.
